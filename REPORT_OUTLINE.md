# Stock Visualization and Business Analysis

## Business Question

We are helping business analysts understand whether major restaurant companies experienced different stock-market reactions surrounding the July 2026 lettuce recall.

## Data and Method

- yfinance data download
- Daily return calculations
- Descriptive statistics
- Correlation analysis
- Monthly summary


## Significant Movements

### CMG

August 4, 2026

CMG declined approximately 9.7% following reports of food-safety concerns and a salmonella investigation involving ingredients supplied to certain Chipotle locations. Investors appeared to react negatively to the potential reputational and operational impact.

### MCD

July 2, 2026

MCD increased approximately 4.16% before the July 17 lettuce recall announcement. The movement appears to be related to investor demand for established restaurant companies and broader market sentiment rather than the recall itself.

### CAVA

August 12–13, 2025

CAVA declined approximately 16.6% after releasing second-quarter 2025 earnings results. Investors reacted negatively to slower same-restaurant sales growth and management's decision to reduce its full-year sales growth forecast despite continued revenue growth.

Supporting evidence included elevated trading volume, reduced sales guidance, and weaker-than-expected same-restaurant sales growth.

## Findings

### Finding 1

CAVA and CMG had the strongest positive correlation (0.465), indicating that their daily returns moved more similarly than the other stock pairs.

### Finding 2

CAVA experienced the highest volatility during the analysis period (Standard Deviation = 4.07%), while McDonald's experienced the lowest volatility (Standard Deviation = 1.54%).

### Finding 3

Major stock movements were associated with different business events:
- CMG: food-safety concerns
- MCD: investor sentiment and defensive-stock demand
- CAVA: earnings guidance reductions and slowing same-restaurant sales growth


## Recommendations

- Investors should consider both company-specific news and broader market conditions when evaluating restaurant stocks.
- Earnings guidance revisions can significantly affect stock prices even when revenue continues to grow.
- Food-safety issues can create substantial short-term volatility because of reputational risk.

## Limitations

## Limitations

- Correlation does not imply causation.
- Stock price movements may be influenced by broader economic and market factors.
- Multiple business events can occur simultaneously, making it difficult to identify a single cause of a stock movement.
- The CAVA event occurred outside the primary project analysis window and should be verified for consistency with project requirements.
